# Archives

Compressed archives, disk images, and installer packages. Often stale — left behind after extracting their contents or running an installer.

## Details

Extensions classified as archives: `zip`, `tar`, `gz`, `tgz`, `bz2`, `xz`, `7z`, `rar`, `dmg`, `iso`, `pkg`, `jar`, `war`, `deb`, `rpm`.

`.dmg` and `.pkg` files in `~/Downloads` are particularly common offenders — most installers leave the original sitting around even after you've moved the app to Applications.
