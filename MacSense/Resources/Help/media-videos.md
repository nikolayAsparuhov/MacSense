# Videos

All video files MacSense found under your home folder. Usually the largest single category on a Mac because video bitrates dwarf everything else.

## Details

Extensions classified as video: `mp4`, `mov`, `mkv`, `avi`, `webm`, `m4v`, `flv`, `wmv`, `mpeg`, `mpg`, `3gp`, `ts`.

The card shows the top 5 largest videos with their on-disk size. Clicking the card opens the full list, sorted biggest-first, where you can move individual files to Trash.

System Library video assets and items inside protected app bundles are excluded.
