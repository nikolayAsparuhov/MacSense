# Audio

Music tracks, podcasts, voice memos, and any other audio file in your home folder.

## Details

Extensions classified as audio: `mp3`, `flac`, `wav`, `m4a`, `aac`, `ogg`, `opus`, `aiff`, `alac`, `wma`, `aif`.

Lossless formats (`flac`, `wav`, `aiff`, `alac`) are an order of magnitude larger than compressed equivalents. If your audio total is high, check whether you're keeping both lossless masters and compressed copies of the same library.
