# Documents

Word processor files, PDFs, spreadsheets, presentations, and ebooks.

## Details

Extensions classified as documents: `pdf`, `docx`, `doc`, `pages`, `rtf`, `txt`, `md`, `odt`, `xlsx`, `xls`, `numbers`, `pptx`, `ppt`, `key`, `keynote`, `epub`, `mobi`, `csv`.

Documents are usually small individually but accumulate quickly in `~/Documents`, Desktop, and Downloads. A large PDF or Keynote with embedded media is often the outlier worth deleting.
